How ResumeMetric Calculates Your ATS Score

Modeled directly after Resume Worded standard

1. Impact (40% Weight)Heaviest Factor

Measures if your bullets describe business accomplishments instead of passive duties. Rewards quantifiable metrics (%, $, scale) in ≥40% of bullets and strong action verbs, while penalizing verb repetition and clichés.

2. Brevity (25% Weight)Scan Optimization

Evaluates total word count (250-500 words optimal for 1 page fresher resume) and flags bullet lengths that are too short (<9 words) or run-ons (>34 words).

3. Style & ATS Compatibility (20% Weight)Robot Readability

Checks for direct contact info (Email, Phone, LinkedIn, GitHub) and canonical section headers (Experience, Education, Skills, Projects).

4. Relevancy (15% Weight)Role Alignment

Compares technical skills and keywords from your target job description against your resume text.
